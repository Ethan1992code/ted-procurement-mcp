# TED data reuse notice

This project queries procurement notices published on Tenders Electronic Daily (TED), managed by the Publications Office of the European Union.

TED's current legal notice states that, unless otherwise noted, procurement notices published in the Supplement to the Official Journal of the European Union may be freely reused for commercial or non-commercial purposes. SIMAP/TED editorial website content is generally CC BY 4.0, and SIMAP metadata is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Additional rights may apply to third-party works or identifiable private individuals.
